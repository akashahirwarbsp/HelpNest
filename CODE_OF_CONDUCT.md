# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and respectful experience for everyone, regardless of experience level, identity, background, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while addressing impact.
- Accept feedback graciously.
- Focus on what is best for the community.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, insults, personal attacks, discrimination, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, and other conduct that makes participation unsafe are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this Code and may block participation in serious cases. Report concerns privately to the repository maintainer listed in the repository settings.

This document is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant community standards.
